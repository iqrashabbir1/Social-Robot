## Evidence Boundary

The repository distinguishes repository-implemented baselines, benchmark-supported enhanced experiments, simulation-supported modules, prototype/scaffold modules, and future clinical-validation requirements. It does not provide clinical deployment evidence, ethics approval, live wearable integration, or clinician-validated assisted-living pilot results.
