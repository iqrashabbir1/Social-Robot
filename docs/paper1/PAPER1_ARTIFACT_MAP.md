## Evidence Boundary

The repository distinguishes implemented real baselines, manuscript-facing enhanced benchmark outputs, simulation-supported modules, prototype modules, and future clinical-validation requirements. It does not provide clinical deployment evidence, ethics approval, live wearable integration, or clinician-validated assisted-living pilot results.
