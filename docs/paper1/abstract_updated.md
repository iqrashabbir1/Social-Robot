# Updated Abstract

This paper presents a simulation-first, ROS2-compatible, technically validated, and preliminary framework for cognitive caregiving robots. The implementation is organized around three measurable case studies. CS1 evaluates a digital-twin backbone through ROS2-compatible topic interfaces, timestamped event logging, playback grounding, and disturbance analysis. CS2 builds a multimodal sensing pipeline that aligns video, audio, and robot-context streams into fixed windows while explicitly tracking synchronization quality and modality availability, and augments the synthetic study with a small locally collected pilot real-anchor session. CS3 preserves the repository’s real visual baseline and adds preliminary classical, XGBoost, deep-fusion, and transformer-style benchmark paths for multimodal emotion recognition.

The current work does not claim clinical validation, live robot deployment, or caregiving effectiveness. Instead, it contributes a reproducible technical substrate for later simulator studies, richer multimodal dataset collection, and future ROS2-integrated robot experiments. Results are explicitly labeled as synthetic, pilot real-anchor, playback-grounded, or mixed.
