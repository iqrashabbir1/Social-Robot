# Paper 1 Scope Update

Paper 1 is now explicitly framed as:
- simulation-first
- ROS2-compatible
- technically validated
- preliminary
- a foundation for future real-world caregiving studies

## What Paper 1 validates
- a reproducible digital-twin and synchronization backbone
- ROS2-compatible topic structure and playback-grounded analysis
- preliminary multimodal benchmarking infrastructure
- a small pilot real-anchor collection and loading path

## What Paper 1 does not validate
- live clinical deployment
- real-world caregiving effectiveness
- long-term human-robot interaction outcomes
- hospital or assisted-living operational readiness

## Reviewer-facing position
The paper should be presented as a strong technical framework paper with limited but honest real grounding, not as an outcome paper.
