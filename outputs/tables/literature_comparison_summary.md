# Literature Comparison Summary

The comparison matrix uses a qualitative 0-3 capability rubric rather than fabricated empirical performance.

## Why A8 Is Stronger
- A8 is the only approach that jointly covers emotion awareness, health-risk prediction, medication reasoning, ROS2 plus digital twin support, explainability, HITL governance, privacy awareness, telepresence, and real-world readiness.
- A5 preserves the repository's current implemented baseline value, but it remains a narrow affect benchmark rather than a caregiving stack.
- A1 and A4 support monitoring, but they do not close the loop with explanation, dialogue, and oversight.
- A6 and A7 add social reasoning or explainability, but neither fully integrates multimodal sensing, deployment realism, and privacy-aware governance.
- A3 supports reproducible robotics evaluation but lacks the full cognitive and care reasoning layer.

## Publishable Gap
The strongest publication opportunity is not a claim of best performance. It is the explicit transition from single-task or partial-stack systems toward a modular, benchmarkable, closed-loop caregiving framework with:
- preserved baseline evidence
- simulation-backed systems evaluation
- clear ablation paths
- deployment-aware constraints

## Interpretation Note
These scores represent literature-aligned archetype coverage, not measured benchmark results from this repository.
